Revive

When a woman who has spent her life avoiding people who need her falls for a man reeling from his parents’ sudden deaths, she must decide whether she can finally stay and be needed instead of running.

Writing

The script

Point of view
Renata “Rena” Hernandez, 28 — a software engineer in Monterey, California

Characters

  • Renata “Rena” Hernandez, 28 — software engineer, Monterey. Guarded, precise, self-sufficient. Pushes people away before they can get close enough to leave.
  • Noel Anderson, 30 — forestry conservationist, living alone in his late grandmother’s house. So naturally open and giving that he pulls people deeper than they are ready to go — the mirror of Rena’s flaw, and what triggers her retreat.

Why it is low risk

A short, contained romantic drama with a small cast and a low location count, written as a proof of concept for a feature-length version. Currently in redraft.
